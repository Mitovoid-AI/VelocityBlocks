New in 1.3.0: chase a ghost and share your best lap.
- Race one target ghost at a time: Gold, then the Author, then your best. Its line is on the road for the whole lap, with a live gap and your split at every checkpoint.
- Author times are now set by an AI driver that practised every track. They are tougher.
- Replays show gas, brake, steering and nitro, and the line you drove.
- Share a replay as a video, or as a link your friends can watch and race.

## What's new

### Chase a ghost, follow its line
- **One target ghost at a time:** **Gold** until you win gold, then the **Author**, then your **personal best**. Pause > **Chase** picks a different one.
- **Its line on the road** for the whole lap, coloured by what it does there: green on the throttle, yellow lifting, red braking, violet on nitro.
- **A live gap** to the ghost (green when you're ahead, red when behind), with your gap logged at every checkpoint.
- **The ghost's name tag** stays on screen even when the ghost is behind you, so it never just disappears.

### New author times
- **Set by a pro AI driver** that practised every track for the Car, Bike and Truck. They are about 20-24% faster than before (A01: 10.00 s, was 12.86 s), and the gold, silver and bronze times follow from them.
- **Existing medals may change:** they are now judged against these times. Your records and unlocked tracks are untouched.

### Replays you can read
- **Telemetry** in every replay: gas and brake %, steering, nitro, speed, the clock and your splits.
- **Line: driven** shows the path you actually took; **Line: ideal** shows the racing line, to compare.
- **▶ Best lap** on every campaign card opens your best lap's replay without racing first.
- **Author lap** on the results screen shows how the author time was driven.

### Share your best
- **Share video:** records the replay with its telemetry and opens the share sheet (WhatsApp and the like).
- **Share run:** sends a small link that replays your exact lap in your friend's game, marked **verified** when it matches your time. They can tap **Race it** to race against you.
- **Opening a link:** run links open in the app (choose Velocity Blocks in "Open with"), or use Menu > **Open shared run** and paste one.

## Good to know
- **Best laps set before 1.3.0** replay without the pedal and steering telemetry, and can't be shared as a run link. Video works for them, and new best laps have everything.
- **Video format:** MP4 on most phones. On older Android WebViews it's WebM, which WhatsApp sends as a file.
- **Run links in a browser** play in the web game once the website is updated to 1.3.0.
